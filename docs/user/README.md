# The Booking Assistant

The Booking Assistant is an AI helper inside Moodle. You tell it, in your own words, what you
want to do with your bookings and courses, and it does it for you — or answers your question.

You don't need to learn any commands. Just write naturally, in your own language; the assistant
replies in the same language.

## How to open it

Open the assistant from the **wand icon** in the top navigation bar, or from a Booking activity.
Type your request into the chat box and send it.

## What it can help you with

- **Create and edit booking options** — new sessions, courses, events, slot bookings; change
  dates, prices, places, the teacher, the description or the header image.
- **Manage participants** — book or enrol people into an option, look up who is booked, search
  options and read their details.
- **Set up a Booking activity** — configure the instance, create or update booking rules from
  templates.
- **Build your course** — add activities (a page, link, label, folder, forum, a Booking, a
  quiz), and generate quiz questions from a PDF.
- **Answer questions** — explain how something works, or search the documentation for you (this
  help is what you are reading now).
- **Diagnose problems** — "why can't this student book?", "why was this booking cancelled?",
  "why can't this user see the activity?", and similar.

What you are allowed to do depends on your role in the course — the assistant can only do what
you yourself are permitted to do.

## Two things worth knowing

1. **It shows you what it will do, and asks first.** Before it changes anything, the assistant
   presents a preview of the change and waits for your confirmation. Read-only questions are
   answered straight away.
2. **It works in your language.** Ask in German, English, or another language — the reply comes
   back in the same language.
