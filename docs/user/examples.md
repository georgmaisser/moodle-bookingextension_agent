# Example requests

You don't have to phrase things exactly like this — the assistant understands free text. These
are just to show the range of what you can ask. Write in whatever language you prefer.

## Creating and editing bookings

- "Create a new booking option called *Intro to First Aid* on 5 March at 14:00, 12 places."
- "Add a course on Mondays 09:00–11:00 for the next six weeks."
- "Set the price of *Intro to First Aid* to 20 €, students 10 €."
- "Change the teacher of the Tuesday session to Maria Huber."
- "Move the *First Aid* session to 12 March and add 5 more places."
- "Add a header image to this option." *(then attach the image)*

## Participants

- "Book Maria Huber and Tom Berger into *Intro to First Aid*."
- "Who is booked on the Tuesday session?"
- "Show me the details of *Intro to First Aid*."
- "Find all options about *first aid*."

## Setting up the activity and rules

- "Configure this Booking activity so cancellations are allowed up to 2 days before."
- "Create a reminder rule from the template that emails participants one day before the session."

## Building your course

- "Add a page called *Course information* to the *Materials* section."
- "Add a link to https://example.org/handbook labelled *Handbook*."
- "Add a quiz called *Week 1 check* to this course."
- "Generate quiz questions from this PDF." *(then attach the PDF)*

## Asking and diagnosing

- "How do waiting lists work?"
- "Why can't this student book the Tuesday session?"
- "Why was Tom Berger's booking cancelled?"
- "Why can't this user see the activity?"

## Tips

- **Be specific about which option/activity you mean** — by its name. If several match, the
  assistant will ask you to pick.
- **Attach files** (image, PDF) when the task needs them; the assistant will tell you when it
  expects one.
- **You can say "no"** at the confirmation step, or refine your request and try again.
