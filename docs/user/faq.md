# FAQ

## Does it change things without asking?

No. Before any change (creating or editing an option, booking a user, adding an activity, …)
the assistant shows you a preview and waits for your confirmation. Only read-only questions are
answered immediately, without a confirmation step.

## What is it allowed to do?

Exactly what **you** are allowed to do. The assistant acts with your permissions in the current
course or activity — it cannot do anything you could not do yourself through Moodle.

## In which language does it answer?

In the language you write in. Ask in German and you get a German reply; ask in English and you
get English. You can switch languages between messages.

## Can it see other people's personal data?

It only surfaces information you are already entitled to see, and personal data is masked in the
saved conversation. If you ask about a user you have no relationship with, the assistant will not
expose their contact details.

## It picked the wrong option / course. What now?

Name the option or activity more specifically (its exact title). If several items match, the
assistant asks you to choose rather than guessing. You can also cancel at the confirmation step
and rephrase.

## Can it remember my preferences?

Yes — you can ask it to remember a preference ("always set the default place to 20"), and ask it
to forget it again later. These notes are stored for your account only.

## Something went wrong / it couldn't do it

The assistant tells you what went wrong in plain language. Common reasons: you lack the
permission for that action, a required detail was missing or ambiguous, or an attached file was
expected. Adjust your request and try again.

## Can I ask it how a feature works?

Yes. Ask "how do X work?" and it searches this documentation and gives you a short answer with
the relevant excerpt. You can ask it to read more if you want the full detail.
